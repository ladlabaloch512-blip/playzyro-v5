How Squads Work

Captain creates

The captain creates the squad and receives its join code.

Players join by code

Members join with the squad's join code while logged in.

4 to 5 players

A squad holds 4 to 5 players, and needs at least 4 members to apply.

Apply together

The squad applies from the tournament page; the admin reviews and assigns squads.
